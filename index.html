Frequently asked questions

See all FAQs ›

Straight answers about how this directory works and what the data does — and doesn't — tell you.

Is this directory affiliated with Minnesota DHS?
No. This site is built from Minnesota DHS's public Community Residential Setting (CRS) licensing lookup, but it is an independent directory — not operated, endorsed, or reviewed by the Minnesota Department of Human Services.
Is it free to search?
Yes. Searching, filtering by county or service type, and viewing every one of the 945 listed locations is free and does not require an account. Filtering runs entirely in your browser against the published dataset.
About this prototype

This is a working demo built from real Minnesota DHS licensing data, scoped to the 340-operator launch cohort (all operators with fewer than 9 licensed locations). Every location, address, phone number, license number, and license status shown is real public licensing data. Nothing has been claimed yet — there are no photos, descriptions, or reviews beyond what DHS provides, by design. Search and filtering run entirely in your browser against a static data file; there is no backend, login, or payment processing in this build. The sponsored banner above is a working example of a paid ad slot (AD_SLOT_HOME_FEATURED_CAROUSEL in the platform spec) — the three example cards are illustrative, not paying customers.
